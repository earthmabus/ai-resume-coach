# Product Roadmap

> **Status:** Canonical planning document
> **Audience:** Product and engineering leadership
> **Purpose:** Record product direction without redefining infrastructure requirements
> **Owner:** Product Leadership
> **Related documents:** [System capabilities](SYSTEM_CAPABILITIES.md), [documentation portal](../README.md)

This roadmap is directional. Items below are not implemented capabilities, committed dates, or infrastructure requirements unless promoted through product and engineering decisions.

## Product direction

- Improve coaching workflows
- Improve evidence-based resume guidance
- Expand role-comparison and tailoring quality
- Improve useful product analytics with privacy-aware measurement
- Evaluate collaboration and integrations only when user value and ownership are clear

Platform work should follow demonstrated product, security, reliability, or operating needs. It must not introduce new paid services or architectural complexity merely to satisfy this roadmap.
